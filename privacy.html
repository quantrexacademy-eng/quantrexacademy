Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Quantrex Academy is a professional coaching institute for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET, NDA and school exams. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect when you use www.quantrexacademy.com and the official Android app Quantrex Academy on Google Play (package com.quantrexacademy.app).

Google sign-in is optional and safe. If you tap Continue with Google, Google may email you a standard security note that you signed in to quantrexacademy.com. That mail is sent by Google, not by us. We only receive your name and email so we can open your Quantrex student account. We never see your Google password and we never post from your Gmail.

Data we collect

  • Account: name, class, state, district, exam interest, email and/or mobile when you register or log in.
  • Study: practice progress, tests, bookmarks — so your work is saved.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We store order IDs to unlock the course you bought. We do not store card numbers.

How we use it

Only to run your Quantrex Academy account: show the right class and exam content, call you about Maths class if you asked, process a purchase, and improve teaching. We do not sell student data. We do not use your Google account for advertising.

Who helps us run the portal

  • Google Firebase — secure login (email, mobile OTP, Google)
  • Razorpay — course fees
  • Vercel — website hosting

Parents and students

This portal is for students preparing for competitive exams, generally 13 years and above. A parent may contact us any time about an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
