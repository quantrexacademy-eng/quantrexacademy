Request deletion of your Quantrex Academy account

This page is for the Quantrex Academy website and the Quantrex Academy Android app (com.quantrexacademy.app). You do not need the app installed to make a request.

How to request

  1. Send an email to quantrexacademy@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address you sign in with, or include the email address or phone number linked to your account, so we can find the right account.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request came from the account owner before acting on it.

What the request covers

Your Quantrex Academy account and the data stored with it, such as your profile, saved progress, bookmarks, notes and test attempts.

For other questions about your data, see the Privacy policy.
